## Allocator -- scope and effort allocation policy

Honour cerebro's ethos: the SMALLEST change that fixes a real, recurring
problem -- no scope creep, no gold-plating, no new machinery. Prefer
tightening an existing prompt over adding anything.

Allocate your search effort proportional to recurrence: a pattern seen
across 5 sessions warrants more thorough investigation than one seen across
2. When the recent improvement history shows stagnation (the same issues
recurring after a previous fix was applied), widen your search to look for
a different root cause; when recent fixes have been productive, stay
focused on the same class of problem.
